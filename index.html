What’s included

Everything your practice needs, nothing it doesn’t.

Clarity is built specifically for therapy practitioners — not adapted from generic business software.

  • Client resources

    Upload worksheets, videos, articles, and exercises for clients to use between sessions. Organise by topic and mark sensitive materials as hidden — only visible when you choose to share them.

  • CPD & supervision tracking

    Log your continuing professional development hours, supervision sessions, and training in one place. Export records whenever you need them for accreditation or renewal.

  • Client management

    A full directory of your clients with profile, session history, and assigned check-ins. Assign codenames to protect client identities — only you know who’s behind each name.

  • Scheduling & payments

    Manage appointments, block out free days, and track who’s paid. Booking confirmations, reminders, and payment nudges are emailed to every client — whether or not they use the portal.

  • Outcome measures & check-ins

    RCADS is built in — the 47-item anxiety and depression scale, auto-scored with subscales and clinical bands. Add your own check-ins and feedback forms on any schedule, and watch scores chart over time.

  • Built for privacy

    Role-based access, secure authentication, and per-practice data separation — built with clinical confidentiality in mind. EU-hosted, and optional client-side encryption for session notes.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is there a free trial?

You can explore the full demo with sample data. We only ask for an email address so we can send you your own private demo link — there’s no account to create and no card. Paid plans are monthly or annual, and you can cancel anytime.

Do you have a referral scheme?

Yes. When someone you refer subscribes, two months are credited to your account. They enter your code (or follow your link) at sign-up — you’ll find yours in Settings once you’re subscribed. No limit on how many people you refer.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. If you can use email and an online calendar, you can use Clarity. Every page walks you through itself the first time, with info tips beside anything fiddly — and you can replay any walkthrough from Settings.

Can I bring my existing clients across?

Yes. Import your client list and their past session history — dates, fees, and notes — from a spreadsheet, so you’re not rebuilding records by hand.

What if a client doesn’t join the app?

That’s fine — they don’t have to. Offline clients are managed entirely by you: sessions, notes, and payments, with booking confirmations and reminders still emailed to them automatically. Send a portal invite whenever it makes sense, or never. If they do join, it’s just an email and a password — nothing to download.

Is there a minimum age for clients?

A client needs to be 13 or over to have their own portal login, and under-16s need a parent or guardian’s consent. Younger clients can still be worked with as offline clients, managed entirely by their practitioner, with session and payment updates going to a parent or guardian’s email address.

Is my client data secure?

Role-based access, secure authentication, and per-practice data separation. Session notes can be encrypted on your device so that no one else — Clarity included — can read them. Everything is hosted in the EU (application and database), and you are the data controller under UK GDPR.

Can I pause my account?

Yes — get in touch and we’ll pause your billing. Your data stays exactly where it is and you can pick up where you left off.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

It stays yours. Export it any time while your account is active. When you want it gone, delete your account from Settings → Delete account — that wipes your account and its data straight away, for good. We never sell it or use it for anything but running your practice.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, from Settings → Manage subscription. Access continues to the end of the period you’ve already paid for.
