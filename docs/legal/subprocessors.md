## International transfers

Where a subprocessor is based in the US, personal data transfers from the UK are covered by the **UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA)** or the **EU–US Data Privacy Framework** where applicable. Each subprocessor linked above maintains a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) that documents these safeguards.
