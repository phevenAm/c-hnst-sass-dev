## 2. What this policy covers

This policy explains how we collect and use personal data when you use the WithMe platform — either as a practitioner (someone who runs a practice) or as a client (someone invited to use the platform by their practitioner).

**Important:** WithMe is not a clinical records system. Our Terms & Conditions prohibit storing sensitive clinical notes, medical records, or special category health data on the platform. Practitioners retain responsibility for storing clinical records in a compliant system of their own.

## 4. How long we keep your data

- **Active accounts:** We hold your data for as long as your account is active.
- **Deleted accounts:** We delete personal data within 30 days of account deletion, except where we are required to retain it for legal or financial reasons (e.g. billing records, which we keep for 7 years under UK tax law).
- **Logs:** Server and access logs are retained for up to 90 days.

## 5. Who we share your data with

We share data only with the third-party services listed in our [Subprocessor List](/legal/subprocessors) that are necessary to operate the platform. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Where subprocessors are based outside the UK or EEA, transfers are covered by the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) or equivalent safeguards (see the subprocessor list for details).

## 6. Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate data
- **Delete** your data (right to erasure)
- **Restrict** how we process your data
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Data portability** — receive a copy of your data in a machine-readable format
